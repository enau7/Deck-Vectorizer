CardPond is a Magic: The Gathering deck analysis website that uses machine learning to graph and cluster cards by their text boxes.

How to Use

Submit a decklist in the form of an Archidekt link. Cardpond will graph and semantically cluster cards by their text boxes.
Press and hold on a node to highlight the similar cards within the deck. Different colors represent distinct card clusters.
Submit your own decklist to check theme density, or submit someone else's deck to understand which themes are present.
